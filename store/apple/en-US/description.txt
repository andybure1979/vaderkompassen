Weather Compass helps you compare where the forecast best suits your chosen activity.

Choose activities such as walking, cycling, running, golf, fishing, coastal activities, boating, surfing, hiking, skiing, cinema and indoor swimming. View an activity ranking, relevant weather facts and a map of the locations.

Free includes today's forecast, one region at a time and the standard set of locations. Premium includes all available forecast days, multiple regions, an expanded catalogue of about 1,000 locations, cloud sync and an ad-free experience.

Forecast quality, weather data, scores and rankings are the same in Free and Premium.

Weather forecasts are uncertain. Weather Compass does not replace official warnings or local safety information for the sea, mountains, avalanches or bathing water.
