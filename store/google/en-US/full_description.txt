Weather Compass helps you compare where the forecast best suits your chosen activity.

Explore activity-based weather for walking, cycling, running, golf, fishing, coastal activities, boating, surfing, hiking, skiing, cinema and indoor swimming. The app provides rankings, maps, relevant weather facts and factual text based on available forecast data.

Forecasts can change and are not a safety guarantee. Always check official warnings and local information for marine, mountain, avalanche, bathing-water and other hazardous conditions.
